Four pieces, one protocol

Use the SDK on its own, or let the hub route streams between Voicemeeter, phones, Linux machines and microcontrollers.

C99 SDK

Send, receive and mix on anything with a C compiler

Lock-free jitter buffer with clock-drift resampling, a matrix mixer, PING0 discovery and RT packets. Runs on Linux, Windows, Android, ESP32 and Arduino.

OpenWrt hub

A router that routes audio

Fan-out, renaming and volume in LuCI. A tc eBPF fast path forwards inside the kernel.

snd-vban

A sound card for Linux

An ALSA driver: aplay, arecord, PipeWire and JACK talk VBAN with no daemon. Integer-only, timed by a kernel hrtimer.

Kotlin

Android, without the glue

A 128 KB library for four ABIs. read() fills your audio callback straight from the jitter buffer, with no copy and no allocation.

Encrypted when it matters

Plain VBAN trusts every packet on the LAN. Secure VBAN gives each device its own key and a certificate from a CA that lives on your router.

Audited crypto. Monocypher, vendored unmodified. Noise XX handshake, ChaCha20-Poly1305 on every packet.

Routes follow certificates. A spoofed IP address gets nowhere; revoking a device ends its session at once.

Voicemeeter keeps working. The hub decrypts and forwards to plain receivers, so you can secure one device at a time.

Start with a tone

Build the SDK, play a test tone into one terminal and listen in another. Swap the receiver for Voicemeeter or the hub when it works.

bash
# build
$ cmake -S . -B build && cmake --build build -j
# listen on 6980 and play through ALSA
$ ./build/vban_recv -n Stream1 | aplay -f S16_LE -r 48000 -c 2
# in a second terminal: a 440 Hz tone, 64 frames per packet
$ ./build/vban_send -i 127.0.0.1 -n Stream1 -b 64

Going secure

vban_cert makes the CA, device keys and certificates. Add --ca, --cert and --key to send and receive.

On the router

deploy.sh installs the hub and its LuCI page. The Security tab creates the CA and signs devices.

As a sound card

Build kernel/ against your headers and load snd-vban with a destination and a stream name.
